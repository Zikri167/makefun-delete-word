The word I'd delete from the dictionary: **irregardless**.

"Regardless" already means "without regard". Bolting the negative prefix
"ir-" onto a word that is already negative adds nothing: "irregardless"
means exactly the same thing as "regardless", only louder and wronger.
Taken literally it even points the wrong way — "ir-" (not) plus "-less"
(without) gives "not without regard", the opposite of what the speaker
means. A word that cannot agree with itself deserves to go.
